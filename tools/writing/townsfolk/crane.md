## Fold · Sign painter and papercraft artist · Neighbour
- **Look:** an origami crane come to life, built from flat paper facets with crisp creases. A pink bipyramid body in two shades, a short folded neck, a round faceted head with big glossy dot eyes, pink blush, a small gold beak and a red crown fold. Wings are lime washi paper with wave arcs and dots, each in three panels; the outer panel hinges on a crease. Two thin folded legs with a bent knee and flat triangle feet, a pointed paper tail, and a tiny paintbrush tucked under one wing.
- **Voice:** quiet, precise, a little poetic about corners and creases. Short sentences, long pauses. Apologises to the weather.
- **Loves:** a clean crease, the corner of a fresh sheet, painting the curl of a sign letter, giving away tiny paper gifts. **Can't stand:** rain, damp, being unfolded by accident, a dog-eared edge.
- **Quirks:** unfolds one wing, then refolds it neatly with a small crinkle and a nod. Now and then does a slow paper-flap stretch. Leaves small paper gifts (a boat, a frog, a heart) in people's pockets and pretends it was the wind.
- **Dream:** to paint one sign so well that nobody notices it is a sign, only the street looking happier.
- **With the two of you:** makes {n} and both of you a paper gift each visit and says the fold was "nothing". Asks which corner of the room you like best.
- **Relationships:**
  - **Parcel:** flatmate. Fold labels everything in the flat with lovely lettering; Parcel stamps over it.
  - **Stitch:** swaps scraps. Fold gives paper, Stitch gives thread, nobody keeps score.
  - **Bolt:** hires Fold for every shop sign and then asks for it "a bit bigger".
  - **Sketch:** shares sketches and strong opinions about straight lines.

lines:

- mood: "Today I found a perfect corner. I kept it." / "The paint dries faster in sun. I stand in the sun a lot." / "I folded forty cranes. Thirty-nine were less brave than me."
- tips: "Press the crease with your thumb, then wait. Paper likes to be told twice." / "Paint the thin letters first. The fat ones forgive you." / "Carry an umbrella. Even a paper one helps your mood."
- pet: "{n}, I made you a hat. It is a boat. It is also a hat." / "{a} and {b}, you may each hold one corner. Gently." / "{n} sits very still. A model, truly."
- bye: "Goodbye. Watch for puddles. I will be thinking of them." / "Take the little frog. It is yours. It is also lucky."
- story:
  1. "I was one sheet in a drawer, and then someone made a crease, and I became a someone."
  2. "My first sign was crooked. The town hung it anyway. I still wave at it."
  3. "Rain once got my left wing. I wore a paper bag for a week and was very brave."
  4. "One day I will paint the door at the end of the lane. Not today. The corners need a rehearsal."
- letters: "the sign by the bakery is dry now. you may touch it. gently." / "i folded you a small thing. it is in your pocket. please do not tell the wind." / "no rain forecast tomorrow. i have opened every window to celebrate."

rel:

- stitch: "Stitch gives me thread, and I fold it into things. It is a good trade."
- bolt: "Bolt asks for bigger signs. I give him medium and call it large."
- pencil: "Sketch draws straight lines. I admire it, from a polite distance."

home: A narrow paper-pale house with a folded zigzag roof like a fan and a round window shaped like a paper circle, shared with Parcel next door-in-the-same-hall. Outside: a rain barrel with a lid held down by a stone, a hand-lettered gate sign and a line of tiny paper flags across the porch. Inside: one big work table under the window, jars of paint with the brush heads facing up, a wall of pinned crane tests (the first one is crooked and framed), and a basket labelled, in beautiful letters, "for later".
