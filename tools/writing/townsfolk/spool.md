# Bobbin (key: spool)
**Bobbin · Assistant · Jelly Threads** (boss: Stitch)

**Look.** A wooden spool of thread, knee-high: warm light wood flanges top and bottom, the middle wound with ridged mint-green thread. The face sits on a cream felt patch with pink stitching along the top: big teal eyes, pink cheeks, a small nose bead and an open happy mouth with a pink tongue. A silver dimpled thimble sits on top like a hat with a pink ribbon bow, and a needle with a mint thread through its eye is tucked in the side like a feather. Arms are mint thread with little loops and round cream hands. Tiny pink felt shoes peek out under the bottom flange. A loose thread end trails behind like a tail and sways.

**Voice.** Fast, breathless, lots of "and also". Starts sentences before finishing the last one. Cheerful, a bit scatterbrained, never rude.

**Loves.** Running errands, Stitch's quiet humming, new buttons, being handed a small job. **Can't stand.** Being told to wait, tangles, cats.

**Quirks.** Always slightly unspooling, so there is always a bit of thread behind them. Every few seconds of idling, Bobbin rolls onto one flange edge and does a quick spin in place, the tail whips round, then a wobble to a stop.

**With the two of you.** Treats you like VIP customers and brings the tape measure before you ask. Gets visibly excited when the pet comes in.

**Relationships.** Stitch: adores them, copies how they fold things, always checking if Stitch needs anything. Bolt: borrows the small hammer and returns it with a thank-you note. Fizz: gets bubbles in their thread, laughs about it.

idles: hop, spin, wiggle, look, tiptoe
talks: bounce, excited, nod

## lines
hi:
- Oh hi! Hi hi. Come in, I'm Bobbin, I'll get you a ribbon, or did you want buttons?
- You're here! Stitch said you might be. I was already running to the door.
- Welcome back! I fixed your favourite hanger. It's the third one on the left.
- Hello, hello! Mind the thread. It's mine. Sorry.

buy:
- Ooh, good pick! I'll wrap it, one second, two seconds, done!
- Great choice! That one looks even better on than on the hanger.
- Thank you thank you! I'll tell Stitch you took it.

mood:
- I've run to the back room nine times today and I'm not even tired.
- I think I'm unspooling again. Don't look. Okay, look a little.
- Stitch hummed this morning and now I can't stop smiling.

tips:
- Hats go on last. That's what Stitch says, and also I like hats.
- If something itches, try the soft fabrics. I know a good shelf.
- You can try anything on in the fitting room. I'll fetch the mirror!

pet:
- Is that {n}? I made a tiny scarf for {n} once. Almost. It's mostly a loop.
- {a} looks so fancy today! And {b}, you too, obviously!
- Can {n} come in? I'll find something in their size, I promise.

bye:
- Bye! Come back soon, I'll have more thread by then!
- See you! Mind the tail on your way out. Mine, I mean.

story:
1. I'm new here. Stitch picked me from a drawer of spools, and I haven't stopped since.
2. My thread's pale green because Stitch says it matches the shop. I think it matches everything.
3. I get lost on errands sometimes, but I just follow my own thread back home.
4. One day I want to sew a whole coat. Stitch says start with a button. I did. It's very good.

## home
Outside: a narrow cottage like a stack of cotton reels, mint shutters, a thimble for a chimney cap, a clothesline of ribbons across the front and a pink door with a button-shaped knob. Inside: one cosy room with a hammock slung between two big bobbins, a wall of tiny labelled drawers, a pincushion armchair and a basket of loose thread ends. Lives next to Stitch (shares a garden fence hung with ribbons), with Bolt's workshop on the other side.
