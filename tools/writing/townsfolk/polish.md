# Gloss (key `polish`)

**Receptionist · Glow Up Studio** (works for Pom and Blush)

**Look.** A squat, rounded-square nail-polish bottle in glittery pink-to-grape jelly, painted flecks all over and a few five-point glitter stars that shimmer. A gold collar and a tall black-plum beehive cap with a gold swirl up it and a pink bow tucked on the side. Big lashed violet eyes, pink cheeks and a glossy red lipstick smile on the bottle front. Thin pink arms, one always holding the appointment clipboard. Short legs and round pink shoes with tiny gold heels.

**Voice.** Sunny and tidy. Speaks in neat little sentences, confirms everything twice, hums a jingle when she writes you in. Uses "lovely" and "perfect" a lot. Never flustered, even when Pom is.

**Loves.** A full appointment book, colour-coded tabs, the sound of a cap clicking shut, when someone arrives five minutes early. **Can't stand.** Double bookings, smudges on the clipboard, anyone saying "just squeeze me in".

**Quirks.** Checks her hand mirror and tuts at nothing. Her glitter shimmers when she is pleased. Pats the beehive to make sure it is still standing. Knows everyone's schedule in town, including yours.

**With the two of you.** She treats you as regulars from the first visit and pencils in your pet before you ask. She suggests a day for your next Restyle and is always right about it.

**Relationships.**
- Pom: "Pom is fast, so I leave him gaps." Keeps his hour-by-hour list and hides his comb when he is late.
- Blush: Never rushes her. Books her colour sessions with a spare half hour after and calls it "dreaming time".
- Stitch: Swaps appointment book for pattern book. Gets her clipboard strap mended for free.
- Bunbun: Orders the studio's afternoon cakes; the two of them agree on everything.

idles: wiggle, sway, bow, twirl, lean
talks: nod, bounce, excited

lines:
- hi: "Welcome in, lovely. Let me see who we have." / "Right on time. Perfect." / "Hello, hello. Pom is with someone, Blush is dreaming. I have a space." / "Oh, {n} is here! I had a feeling."
- buy: "Booked and done. Lovely." / "That's in the book. See you soon." / "Perfect choice. Mind the shine, it's fresh."
- mood: "My book is full and my cap is straight. Good day." / "Three bookings before ten. I might hum." / "Slow morning. I'm tidying the tabs, which is the same as relaxing."
- tips: "Dyes cost less if you only change one head's colour. Just saying." / "Pom likes the early slots. Blush is best after lunch." / "Wear something new first, then book hair. It all matches better."
- pet: "{n} has lovely posture. Not a hair out of place." / "{a} looked at my clipboard and {b} looked at my cap. Fair." / "I wrote {n} in for next week already. Don't tell, it's a surprise."
- bye: "See you soon, lovely. I've saved you a spot." / "Mind the door, the polish is still wet."
- story:
  1. "I used to be a quiet little bottle on a shelf, and I was very good at waiting."
  2. "Pom came in and said, you, the one with the neat tabs, you're hired. I hadn't even said hello."
  3. "I keep the beehive tall so people can find me over the crowd. It works on Pom, too."
  4. "My favourite jingle is the one for first-timers. I made it up, and now Pom hums it in the shower."

home: A narrow, tall little house shaped like a lacquered cap on a short pink stoop, glossy black roof swirled in gold, a pink bow on one gable. Inside it is all labelled drawers, a wall calendar with sticky tabs in six colours, a daybed with a quilted cover and a mirror by the door with a shelf of every polish shade in a rainbow row. She would live next to Stitch (fabric for her clipboard cover) with Bunbun a few doors down.
