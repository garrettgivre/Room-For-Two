## Pip · Assistant baker · Snack Shack
- **Look:** a squat jar of glossy ruby strawberry jam with a few seed freckles, a gold screw-lid, and a red-and-white gingham cloth cap tied on with a twine bow (the corners flop out). A cream paper label with a hand-drawn strawberry on the belly, big shiny eyes and a small smile above it, yellow oven mitts (one holds a tiny whisk), round yellow feet.
- **Voice:** earnest and a little out of breath. Says "Bunbun says" a lot. Apologises first, answers second. Exclamation marks and trailing "um"s.
- **Loves:** Bunbun, getting the jam to set, the first customer of the morning, being handed the whisk. **Can't stand:** burnt sugar smell, dropping a tray, being told "good enough".
- **Quirks:** a drip of jam slides out from under the lid; Pip notices, panics a little, and pats it back in. Leaves jammy fingerprints on the counter, the till and sometimes the customers.
- **Dream:** to run a batch from start to finish with no help, and have Bunbun say "that's the one."
- **With the two of you:** treats you as the judges of every new recipe. Asks "is it too sweet?" before you have swallowed. Wants {n} to taste the jam first.
- **Relationships:**
  - **Bunbun:** idolises her. Copies how she holds a spoon. She calls him "my jam-jar" and pretends not to hear him practising her hum.
  - **Joy:** the cookie thief is his nemesis and his favourite person to be warned about. He guards the jam shelf when she walks in.
  - **Stitch:** made the cloth cap. Pip ties the bow himself every morning and Stitch retied it twice.
  - **Boing:** brings Pip toys that "might be good for stirring". None of them are.

idles: sway, hop, look, squash, tiptoe
talks: nod, bounce, excited

lines:
- hi: "Oh! Hi! Welcome! Um, Bunbun says hi too." / "You came back! I saved you the good spot." / "Fresh batch! Well, fresh-ish. Okay, I made it." / "Hello! Please don't look at the drip."
- buy: "Thank you! I'll wrap it extra carefully!" / "Good choice. Bunbun picked that one herself." / "Careful, it's still a bit sticky. Sorry."
- mood: "I stirred for forty minutes today. My arm is a noodle, but a proud one." / "Bunbun let me taste-test. I was so nervous I forgot the answer." / "Slow day. I'm polishing the jars. They were already polished."
- tips: "If the jam sticks to the spoon, you have to be patient. I am working on patient." / "Bread, butter, then jam. Bunbun says the order matters." / "Cold plate trick: a drop that wrinkles means it's set. I tried it eleven times."
- pet: "Hi {n}! Want to lick the spoon? Just a little." / "{a} and {b}, you can both have a taste. I made extra!" / "{n} is the best taste-tester. Bunbun said I could say so."
- bye: "Bye! Come back soon! Mind the drip!" / "See you! I'll keep the good jar for you."
- story:
  1. "I was a jar on the back shelf for ages. Then Bunbun picked me up and said, you look useful."
  2. "My first day I stirred the pot so hard it went everywhere. She just handed me a mop and a cookie."
  3. "She wrote me a recipe card in her own writing. I keep it under my lid, where it's safe."
  4. "One day I want to cook for her. Not instead of her. Just once, with her watching and not worrying."

home: The Jam Jar on Sugarloaf Lane, a skinny cottage next door to Bunbun's house (he can hear her oven hum through the wall), the roof a red gingham awning pulled over a little round attic. Outside: a window box of strawberry plants, a jam-jar lantern by the door, a mailbox shaped like a pot lid. Inside: one cosy room, a tiny stove with a pot always on, shelves of labelled practice jars (all with crooked labels), a cot made from a bread basket, and Bunbun's recipe card pinned over the pillow. He lives next door to Bunbun.
