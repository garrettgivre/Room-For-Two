# Loofah (key: sponge) · Assistant · Bubble Salon

**Look.** A plump butter-yellow sea sponge, wider than tall, covered in soft darker pores (the face patch is left smooth). A pink towel twisted into a turban sits on top with a lighter pink band and a swirled knot, and a dollop of white lather peeks out of the knot. Sleepy-happy eyes, rosy cheeks, a small easy smile, short soft arms, and blue flip-flops with orange toe straps on stubby legs. From across the shop it reads as a pink turban on a yellow lump.

**Voice.** Slow, warm, beach-spa calm. Short sentences, lots of "mm" and "no rush, no rush". Hums between lines. Never hurries a sentence and never raises its voice, even when Fizz does.

**Loves.** Warm water, the first bath of the morning, the sound of a tub filling, folding towels while they're still warm, Fizz's good days. **Can't stand.** Cold tiles, being rushed, and anyone calling the shop "busy" in a loud voice.

**Quirks.**
- Every so often it gives itself a slow squeeze. A few bubbles float up out of the turban, wobble, and pop.
- Hums when nobody is talking (the tune changes with the weather).
- Tests the bath water with an elbow, even though it has no elbows to speak of.

**With the two of you.** Treats you like guests who just got home. Offers a warm towel before you've said hello. Remembers which of the two heads likes the water hotter. At higher friendship it saves you the corner spot by the window.

**Relationships.**
- **Fizz (boss):** the only one who can calm him down. A hand on his shoulder and "no rush, Fizz" works every time. Fizz pretends he didn't need it.
- **Pom and Blush:** shares the laundry room with them. Swaps towels for a face mask at the end of the week.
- **Cushy:** the two of them could nap through a thunderstorm. They have, once.
- **Bunbun:** brings warm buns to the bath hall on slow afternoons and eats them in the steam.

idles: sway, squash, look, stretch, rock
talks: sway, nod, lean

## lines
hi:
- "Mm, hello. Come in, come in. No rush."
- "Oh good, you're here. The water's just right."
- "Hello, hello. Want a warm towel?"
- "Morning. Or afternoon? Either way, welcome."

buy:
- "There you go. Take your time with it."
- "Mm, nice choice. Soft and warm."
- "All done. No rush on anything."

mood:
- "Floaty. The good kind of floaty."
- "A bit damp, very happy. Same as always."
- "Humming all day. Can't help it."

tips:
- "Warm water first, then the bubbles. Cold makes everyone grumpy."
- "If Fizz is shouting, wait a minute. He runs out."
- "Bath before bed and {n} sleeps like a stone."

pet:
- "{n} smells like fresh towels today. That's a good sign."
- "{a} wants it hotter, I think. {b} says it's fine. I'll split the difference."
- "Bring {n} by after play. A little soak sorts everything out."

bye:
- "Bye now. No rush coming back, but do."
- "Mm, see you. Stay warm."

story:
1. "I was a bath sponge once, just on the shelf. Fizz picked me up and said, you're staff now."
2. "The turban was Pom's idea. Blush folded it. It's never come undone, not once."
3. "Fizz has big days. I just stand next to him until his bubbles settle. That's the whole job."
4. "My favourite part is closing time. Quiet tubs, steam on the windows, and a hum with nobody listening."

## home
Outside: a squat, rounded cottage the colour of butter, with a pink-and-white striped awning, a porthole window, and a round green door. A stack of folded towels sits by the step and a pair of flip-flops waits on the mat. A little chimney puffs soap bubbles instead of smoke. Inside: one big deep bath in the middle of the room, a hammock, shelves of towels sorted by colour, and a kettle that is always warm. It lives in Loofah's Sunny Hut on Bubble Bay, next to Fizz (close enough to hear him through the wall, far enough to nap).
