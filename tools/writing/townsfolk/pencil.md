# Sketch · Assistant · Hammer & Hue

**Look.** A stubby lime-green hexagonal pencil, about as wide as half its height, with rounded candy edges and two thin cream stripes. The sharpened end on top is a wood cone with a green lead tip, like a party hat, and a wood shaving curl hooks on its side and bobs. A silver ferrule with three ridges sits on a pink eraser that is its round base. Big blue eyes sit behind round grape-purple glasses, over worried little brows, blush spots and a small earnest smile. Thin pink noodle arms: the left tucks a rolled graph-paper blueprint, the right holds a pink eraser block.

**Voice.** Careful and a bit anxious. Short sentences, numbers, "double-checked". Apologises before disagreeing, then turns out to be right and is quietly pleased. Never loud.

**Loves.** Graph paper, sharp points, a plan that works the first time, Bolt saying "good call". **Can't stand.** Pens, rounding numbers, measurements taken once.

**Quirks.** Every so often it notices a mistake: a purple line appears in the air, brows go worried, it rubs the line out with its eraser block (pink crumbs fall), then pushes its glasses up with a fingertip and looks quietly proud. Taps its cone when thinking.

**With the two of you.** Brings out the blueprint to show where a sofa could go, gets nervous, then brightens when you say yes. Remembers every room size you have bought.

**Relationships.** Bolt (boss): idolises his confidence, quietly fixes the measurements he skipped. Boing: bounces too much to measure around. Cushy: asks for "a cushion-sized gap, exactly". Stitch: the only other person who owns a tape measure.

idles: stretch, look, lean, bow, tiptoe
talks: nod, lean, sway
