## Pixel · Co-host · Jelly Arcade
- **Look:** a chunky cube buddy built from 21 glossy candy voxels, cyan at the feet fading to grape on top. A dark screen sits on the front where the face is lit in lime pixels (normal eyes, a blink, ^ ^ when happy, pink pixel hearts when cheering). Stubby block arms, wide feet, a tiny yellow antenna pixel, and a gold +1 coin that bobs over its head.
- **Voice:** deadpan and quiet. Short sentences, dry gamer humour, numbers. Never exclaims. Says "noted" and "GG" in the same flat tone.
- **Loves:** counting tickets, high scores that are exactly round numbers, Game Night, a clean leaderboard. **Can't stand:** lag, uneven ticket stacks, being asked to "show off".
- **Quirks:** the glitch. Every so often its voxels drift apart, jitter, and snap back one step at a time, and Pixel pretends nothing happened. Is secretly the best player in town and will only admit it as "I've had some practice".
- **With the two of you:** counts your tickets out loud, always correctly. Has a hidden score on every cabinet that nobody has beaten, and mentions it only when you ask. Calls you "player one" and "player two" but flatter than Joy does.
- **Joy:** the boss, and Pixel's favourite noise. Joy yells, Pixel keeps score. Gets dragged into every tournament announcement and does the number part.
- **Boing:** Game Night co-host with Joy. Boing bounces, Pixel stands very still next to him. They have a bet on who tires first.
- **Fizz:** he has never beaten Pixel at Bubble Pop and Pixel has never mentioned it. Fizz suspects.
- **Bunbun:** leaves a cookie by the ticket counter on Fridays. It is gone by Saturday and nobody has seen who took it.

home: A narrow pastel-blue cottage stacked like a pile of blocks, with a flat roof and a square window shaped like a screen that glows lime in the evening. A string of ticket-stub bunting hangs over the door and a small cabinet sits on the porch, always on. Inside: one big couch, a wall of framed high scores, a neat jar of tickets sorted by colour, and a rug with a pixel heart. Lives next door to Joy and Boing, close enough to hear Game Night rehearsals through the wall.
