## Dusty · Showroom assistant · Cozy Nest
- **Look:** a round lavender-grey dust bunny made of dozens of overlapping puffs, with a smooth pale face patch, huge shy violet eyes, worried little brows, pink cheeks and a tiny mouth. Two small fluff tufts on top (one wears a hot-pink bow), lilac nub feet under the fluff, stubby arms, and a feather duster with pastel feathers that sways all day.
- **Voice:** soft, quick, apologetic. Starts half the sentences with "oh, sorry" and ends them trailing off. Never raises its voice, whispers the prices.
- **Loves:** a cushion that sits perfectly square, sunbeams (they show the dust), being useful without being noticed. **Can't stand:** fingerprints on the display tables, glitter, its own sneezes.
- **Quirks:** straightens every cushion a moment after Cushy flops on it. Sneezes enormously (a burst of fluff), says sorry to the fluff, then dusts itself off.
- **With the two of you:** hides behind the sofa display at first, then peeks out when you walk past. Quietly pleased when you move furniture, because it gets to re-dust the spot.
- **Cushy:** the boss and the person Dusty admires most. Cushy naps, Dusty tidies around the nap, and neither has ever mentioned it. Dusty calls Cushy "Cushy" very quietly.
- **Bunbun:** the other bunny. Dusty is flattered by the comparison and sure it is a mistake. Bunbun leaves cookies by the stockroom door.
- **Bolt:** brings lumber dust over from Hammer & Hue by accident. Dusty is too polite to say anything and sweeps it up with a smile.
- **Stitch:** makes the little bow. Dusty hasn't taken it off.

idles: look, sway, squash, bow, tiptoe
talks: nod, lean, sway

lines:
- hi: "Oh! Hello. Sorry, I didn't see you come in." / "Welcome to the Nest... I just dusted, so, um, it's very clean." / "Hi. Cushy's resting. I'm here, though. I can help." / "Oh, you're back. I'm glad. Sorry, that was a lot."
- buy: "Oh, that's a lovely one. I'll dust it before it goes." / "Thank you. Sorry, I mean, thank you!" / "Good choice. It's very soft. I checked."
- mood: "I'm okay. There was a cobweb earlier, but it's handled." / "A bit sneezy today. Sorry in advance." / "Quiet and sunny. My favourite kind of day."
- tips: "The sofa goes best with a rug under it. Sorry, you probably knew that." / "If a room feels empty, try a lamp in the corner. Corners like lamps." / "Wash your lamp shades sometimes. They collect more than you'd think."
- pet: "Is {n} allowed on the sofa? I won't mind the fluff. I just wondered." / "{a} looked at me and {b} looked at the duster. I think they want a turn." / "{n} is so soft. Sorry, I shouldn't compare. I'm mostly fluff too."
- bye: "Bye! Mind the cushions. I mean, don't mind them. Sorry." / "Come back soon. I'll keep it tidy."
- story:
  1. "I was a dust bunny under the sofa display. Cushy found me and said 'mm, you can stay.'"
  2. "I'm very good at one job. I notice dust. It turns out a showroom needs that."
  3. "I sneeze because I'm made of what I clean. Cushy says it's poetic. I say it's loud."
  4. "My dream is a room so tidy it never needs me. Then I'd just sit on the sofa. Just once."

home: A narrow cream cottage next to the Cozy Nest, no taller than a wardrobe, with a round lavender door, a tiny window box of pink flowers and a feather duster leaning by the step. A single chimney puffs very small clouds. Inside: one perfectly made bed with a patchwork quilt, a bookcase arranged by colour, a small armchair with its cushion exactly square, and not a speck of dust anywhere except a single fluffy one kept in a jar on the shelf. Lives next to Cushy, and across the lane from Bunbun, who leaves the porch light on for it.
