# The Shopkeepers of Room for Two: a character bible

This is the living reference for the town's residents: who they are, how they talk, what they care about, and how they feel about each other and about the two of you. The in-game dialog (`KBIB` in `index.html`) is written from this. When you add lines, keep them in voice, keep them short (a bubble holds about 110 characters), and keep the town cozy: no villains, no real conflict, only teasing, crushes on colours, and too many cookies.

Placeholders in lines: `{n}` = the pet's name, `{a}` / `{b}` = the pet's two head names.

## The town in one paragraph

A small candy-bright town of eight shops around a fountain plaza. Everyone knows everyone. The keepers are all a little odd in the same gentle way, and they have all decided, without discussing it, that the two of you and your two-headed jelly pet are the most interesting thing to happen here in years. They gossip about each other constantly and affectionately.

## How friendship works

- Each keeper has their own friendship with the two of you (shared: either of you chatting counts).
- The first chat of the day with a keeper: +2. Every purchase in their shop: +1.
- Levels: **New face** (0) → **Regular** (4) → **Friend** (10) → **Good friend** (18) → **Bestie** (30).
- "Tell me about yourself" works through each keeper's story in order. Later chapters wait for a higher level; until then the keeper deflects kindly.
- Higher levels change greetings (warmer, first-name, in-jokes).

## Bunbun · Baker · Snack Shack
- **Look:** an egg-shaped bunny baker with springy ears, a chef's hat and a wooden spoon. Matte, floury.
- **Voice:** warm, fussing, a little flustered. "Sweetie", "hon", food comparisons. Worries you haven't eaten.
- **Loves:** early mornings, the first loaf out of the oven, feeding everyone. **Can't stand:** soggy bottoms, skipped breakfasts.
- **Quirks:** puffs of flour when excited; always burns her tongue on the first cookie of a batch; hums because she can't whistle.
- **Dream:** a town picnic on the plaza every spring.
- **With the two of you:** treats you like family. {n} is "our little {n}". Keeps the first bite of every batch for {n}.

## Fizz · Stylist · Bubble Salon
- **Look:** a curved bean with mismatched eyes and a bubble-bath hairdo that floats off and pops.
- **Voice:** grand and theatrical, but in on the joke. "Darling", "divine", "tragic" (for tiny things).
- **Loves:** warm baths, humid days, compliments. **Can't stand:** dry air (his hair pops), being rushed.
- **Quirks:** bubbles drift off his head when he laughs; narrates his own entrances.
- **With the two of you:** decided on day one that you're his favourite clients. Fusses over {n}'s shine.

## Boing · Toymaker · Toy Box
- **Look:** one big eye, a grin with teeth, a body that's mostly spring.
- **Voice:** bursts of excitement, jumps between thoughts, "okay okay okay", BOING in caps.
- **Loves:** gadgets, stairs (bouncy), loud colours. **Can't stand:** low ceilings, waiting.
- **Quirks:** stretches when excited and bonks the ceiling; names every prototype.
- **With the two of you:** thinks you're the best testers in town. Wants {n} to try everything first.

## Cushy · Furniture · Cozy Nest
- **Look:** a living square cushion with corner tassels and sleepy half-lids.
- **Voice:** slow, soft, "mm", trailing dots. Cozy philosophy. Sometimes dozes mid-sentence.
- **Loves:** naps, rainy afternoons, the perfect sofa corner. **Can't stand:** hard chairs, alarm clocks.
- **Quirks:** fluffs themself when thinking; tassels twitch when dreaming.
- **With the two of you:** thinks your room is "a very good nest". Quietly the best listener in town.

## Bolt · Builder · Hammer & Hue
- **Look:** an egg in overalls with a ribbed hard hat and a pink mallet.
- **Voice:** upbeat, practical, construction puns. "Chief", "let's nail it".
- **Loves:** fresh paint smell, a good level, plans on paper. **Can't stand:** wobbly tables, beige.
- **Quirks:** loses things in his hat; taps things with his mallet "to hear if they're happy".
- **With the two of you:** takes your room personally. Wants to see the walls every time you change them.

## Pom · Antenna stylist · Glow Up Studio
- **Look:** slim and curvy, an afro of curls (each one named), a tool belt.
- **Voice:** fast, trendy, delighted. "Okay but hear me out", "iconic", "obsessed".
- **Loves:** trends, curls, making things pop. **Can't stand:** boring, flat antennae.
- **Quirks:** fluffs the afro to think; talks with the comb.
- **With the two of you:** has a mood board for {a} and {b}. Two heads means two looks, and Pom is thrilled.

## Blush · Colour artist · Glow Up Studio
- **Look:** pear-shaped, paint-flecked, lashes, a beret.
- **Voice:** calm, artsy, a little poetic. Names colours ("apricot sunrise"). Calls people "love".
- **Loves:** sunsets, naming colours, quiet mornings. **Can't stand:** clashing without a reason.
- **Quirks:** dabs colour on anything nearby; keeps a colour journal.
- **With the two of you:** thinks {n} is the best canvas in town and has a page of the journal just for them.

## Stitch · Tailor · Jelly Threads
- **Look:** a ball of yarn with a cream face patch and a tape-measure band.
- **Voice:** gentle and precise, sewing metaphors, "just a smidge". A bit scattered.
- **Loves:** soft wool, finishing touches, patterns that line up at the seams. **Can't stand:** loose threads, moths.
- **Quirks:** unravels a little when flustered; keeps losing needles (they're usually in Stitch).
- **With the two of you:** measures {n} with their eyes every visit. Has a drawer of things "that might fit someday".

## Joy · Arcade host · Jelly Arcade
- **Look:** starry eyes, a speaker-grille tummy, sneakers.
- **Voice:** energetic gamer, kind competitor. "Let's go!", "GG", calls you "player one" and "player two".
- **Loves:** high scores, cheering people on, pinball. **Can't stand:** lag, sore losers.
- **Quirks:** hums chiptune; eyes sparkle on a win.
- **With the two of you:** calls you two "the co-op team". Keeps a spot on the score board warm for {n}.

## Writing new lines
- One idea per line. If a line needs a second breath, split it into two lines (they queue).
- Every keeper should sound different with the name hidden. Read it aloud.
- Gossip is always fond. The worst anyone says is "brave".
- Lines about the pet should notice something (its two heads, its size, its mood) rather than flatter.
- Future: keeper requests (fetch me X, help me decorate), town events (the spring picnic, Game Night, the fair), letters in the Inbox that continue chats.

# The assistants

Every shop has an assistant as well as its keeper, so the shop never closes when the keeper goes home.
Keepers work 7:00-17:00 US Central and are home in the evening; assistants are home in the morning (7:00-13:00) and mind the shop
the rest of the day and night (they help out on the shop floor while the keeper is in, and take the counter when the keeper leaves).
Their dialog (`KBIB`, `ASSIST_LINES`, `RES_T`) comes from the lines in their full sheets, `tools/writing/townsfolk/<key>.md`.

## Pip · Apprentice baker · Snack Shack
- **Look:** a squat jar of glossy ruby strawberry jam with a few seed freckles, a gold screw-lid, and a red-and-white gingham cloth cap tied on with a twine bow (the corners flop out). A cream paper label with a hand-drawn strawberry on the belly, big shiny eyes and a small smile above it, yellow oven mitts (one holds a tiny whisk), round yellow feet.
- **Voice:** earnest and a little out of breath. Says "Bunbun says" a lot. Apologises first, answers second. Exclamation marks and trailing "um"s.
- **Loves:** Bunbun, getting the jam to set, the first customer of the morning, being handed the whisk. **Can't stand:** burnt sugar smell, dropping a tray, being told "good enough".
- **Quirks:** a drip of jam slides out from under the lid; Pip notices, panics a little, and pats it back in. Leaves jammy fingerprints on the counter, the till and sometimes the customers.
- **Dream:** to run a batch from start to finish with no help, and have Bunbun say "that's the one."
- **With the two of you:** treats you as the judges of every new recipe. Asks "is it too sweet?" before you have swallowed. Wants {n} to taste the jam first.
- **Relationships:**
  - **Bunbun:** idolises her. Copies how she holds a spoon. She calls him "my jam-jar" and pretends not to hear him practising her hum.
  - **Joy:** the cookie thief is his nemesis and his favourite person to be warned about. He guards the jam shelf when she walks in.
  - **Stitch:** made the cloth cap. Pip ties the bow himself every morning and Stitch retied it twice.
  - **Boing:** brings Pip toys that "might be good for stirring". None of them are.

## Loofah · Spa helper · Bubble Salon
**Look.** A plump butter-yellow sea sponge, wider than tall, covered in soft darker pores (the face patch is left smooth). A pink towel twisted into a turban sits on top with a lighter pink band and a swirled knot, and a dollop of white lather peeks out of the knot. Sleepy-happy eyes, rosy cheeks, a small easy smile, short soft arms, and blue flip-flops with orange toe straps on stubby legs. From across the shop it reads as a pink turban on a yellow lump.
**Voice.** Slow, warm, beach-spa calm. Short sentences, lots of "mm" and "no rush, no rush". Hums between lines. Never hurries a sentence and never raises its voice, even when Fizz does.
**Loves.** Warm water, the first bath of the morning, the sound of a tub filling, folding towels while they're still warm, Fizz's good days. **Can't stand.** Cold tiles, being rushed, and anyone calling the shop "busy" in a loud voice.
**Quirks.**
- Every so often it gives itself a slow squeeze. A few bubbles float up out of the turban, wobble, and pop.
- Hums when nobody is talking (the tune changes with the weather).
- Tests the bath water with an elbow, even though it has no elbows to speak of.
**With the two of you.** Treats you like guests who just got home. Offers a warm towel before you've said hello. Remembers which of the two heads likes the water hotter. At higher friendship it saves you the corner spot by the window.
**Relationships.**
- **Fizz (boss):** the only one who can calm him down. A hand on his shoulder and "no rush, Fizz" works every time. Fizz pretends he didn't need it.
- **Pom and Blush:** shares the laundry room with them. Swaps towels for a face mask at the end of the week.
- **Cushy:** the two of them could nap through a thunderstorm. They have, once.
- **Bunbun:** brings warm buns to the bath hall on slow afternoons and eats them in the steam.

## Tock · Stock keeper · Toy Box
- **Look:** a vintage tin wind-up toy come to life. A chubby egg of painted teal tin with a red stripe band and little brass rivets, a cream face plate with big round eyes and a small clockwork smile, two round dial ears with ticking needles, red tin shoes, cream round hands with red cuffs, and a big brass butterfly key on its back that turns while it moves. Glossy paint, a touch of metal sheen.
- **Voice:** quiet, exact, a little formal. Numbers and counts ("Three, actually"). Says "tick" when thinking. Never raises its voice, which makes Boing's shouting funnier.
- **Loves:** a full shelf count, labels, matching pairs, the sound of a clean spring. **Can't stand:** unlabelled boxes, things put back in the wrong bin, being rushed mid-count.
- **Quirks:** slowly winds down (moves slower, droops, ears sag, key stops), then reaches back, cranks its own key and perks up with a tick tick. Ticks quietly when it thinks. Keeps the inventory in its head and corrects Boing's numbers gently.
- **With the two of you:** treats you as valued regulars and remembers what {n} has played with. Quietly pleased when you put a toy back where it lives.
- **Boing:** boss and opposite. Boing makes the toys, Tock makes sure they get found again. It winds Boing down with a cup of tea (Boing winds back up in nine seconds).
- **Bolt:** orders bolts and brackets from him, the two of them speak in part numbers.
- **Stitch:** swaps notes on tiny stitches and gears, both love a neat seam.
- **Joy:** keeps her high scores written in a small notebook, and hands her a fresh tin of capsule tokens.

## Dusty · Showroom helper · Cozy Nest

Two small fluff tufts on top (one wears a hot-pink bow), lilac nub feet under the fluff, stubby arms, and a feather duster with pastel feathers that sways all day.
- **Voice:** soft, quick, apologetic. Starts half the sentences with "oh, sorry" and ends them trailing off. Never raises its voice, whispers the prices.
- **Loves:** a cushion that sits perfectly square, sunbeams (they show the dust), being useful without being noticed. **Can't stand:** fingerprints on the display tables, glitter, its own sneezes.
- **Quirks:** straightens every cushion a moment after Cushy flops on it. Sneezes enormously (a burst of fluff), says sorry to the fluff, then dusts itself off.
- **With the two of you:** hides behind the sofa display at first, then peeks out when you walk past. Quietly pleased when you move furniture, because it gets to re-dust the spot.
- **Cushy:** the boss and the person Dusty admires most. Cushy naps, Dusty tidies around the nap, and neither has ever mentioned it. Dusty calls Cushy "Cushy" very quietly.
- **Bunbun:** the other bunny. Dusty is flattered by the comparison and sure it is a mistake. Bunbun leaves cookies by the stockroom door.
- **Bolt:** brings lumber dust over from Hammer & Hue by accident. Dusty is too polite to say anything and sweeps it up with a smile.
- **Stitch:** makes the little bow. Dusty hasn't taken it off.

## Sketch · Planner · Hammer & Hue

The sharpened end on top is a wood cone with a green lead tip, like a party hat, and a wood shaving curl hooks on its side and bobs. A silver ferrule with three ridges sits on a pink eraser that is its round base. Big blue eyes sit behind round grape-purple glasses, over worried little brows, blush spots and a small earnest smile. ## Gloss · Front desk · Glow Up Studio

# Where everyone lives

Three neighbourhoods off the town map (`HOODS`, `HOUSES`; each house is visitable when someone is home and has invited you:
Friend for keepers, Regular for assistants, announced by an Inbox note). Roommates share working hours so they are home together.

- **Sugarloaf Lane** (bakery smells and nap-time quiet): Bunbun (The Warm Loaf), Pip next door (The Jam Jar), Cushy, Dusty. Two empty lots.
- **Bubble Bay** (spa steam and style): Fizz, Loofah, Stitch; Pom & Blush share a house (best friends and co-owners); Gloss & Bobbin are flatmates. Two empty lots.
- **Playhouse Hill** (games, gadgets, building): Boing & Joy share the Game Night house; Tock & Sketch are a couple (they met comparing rulers); Bolt; Pixel. Three empty lots.
- Later additions: Sugarloaf Lane also has Tutti with her niece and nephew, the muffin twins Scone & Crumb (The Sundae Cottage), and Dr. Patch's little clinic; Bubble Bay has Beacon's lighthouse cottage; Playhouse Hill has Parcel & Fold (flatmates, The Paper Post) and Cobble's pebble cottage.
- **Lantern Meadow** (wildflowers, lamplight, the mayor's porch): Mayor Marsh, Dewey, Lumi & Nimbus (housemates), Posy & Prickles (a couple, The Potted Lodge), Echo. One lot left in each neighbourhood, two in the Meadow.

Empty lots ("Someone new soon!") are for townsfolk who move in later. New residents need: a KEEPERS model, a RESIDENTS entry, KBIB lines, a HOUSES entry and a house.

# The townsfolk

Neighbours with their own jobs and routines (not shop staff). Their doors are open whenever they are home; each is out on their rounds
for part of the day (`RESIDENTS[k].out` hours, US Central, `away` says where). Full sheets with every line: `tools/writing/townsfolk/<key>.md`.

## Mayor Marsh · Mayor
- **Look:** a plump, pillowy marshmallow cylinder with rounded edges and a lightly toasted golden top. A little black top hat with a pink band (tipped a touch to one side), round gold spectacles, a curly brown moustache, a pink bow tie, a pink-and-lime sash across the belly with a gold rosette medal, stubby arms in white mitts, shiny black shoes. The right mitt holds a small wooden gavel.
- **Voice:** pompous in the sweetest way. Full sentences, announcements, "ahem" before anything important. Calls everyone "dear resident". Deflates into a mumble when he is touched.
- **Loves:** ceremonies, ribbon cuttings, the first of anything, remembering birthdays (all of them), a speech with three parts. **Can't stand:** hot afternoons (he gets a bit sticky), anyone cutting a ribbon before he has finished the speech, an empty guest book.
- **Quirks:** practises a speech: clears his throat into a fist, puffs his moustache up like a pigeon, then gives a tiny bow to nobody. Taps the gavel on his palm when pleased. Gets sticky in hot weather and apologises to every handrail.
- **Dream:** to cut a ribbon so grand that the scissors have to be fetched from Bolt's workshop.
- **With the two of you:** treats you as the town's founding couple and the guests of honour at everything. Knows both your birthdays and mentions them a month early. Wants {n} to be named Citizen of the Month, every month.
- **Relationships:**
  - **Bunbun:** brings him a warm bun at ten, and he declares it a municipal holiday each time.
  - **Bolt:** lends the giant ribbon scissors and always checks he gets them back.
  - **Fizz:** the only one who laughs at every joke in the speeches, so he is invited to everything.

## Parcel · Mail carrier
- **Look:** a chunky cardboard box creature, warm kraft brown with a tape stripe over the top and folded flaps along the bottom. The face is an address-label patch (cream paper, a pink stamp with a heart in the corner, a faint postmark and two address lines), with big blue eyes, pink cheeks and a happy open mouth. A heart sticker, the FRAGILE kind with no words, sits on the side and another on the back. A sky-blue postal cap with a lime brim and a heart badge sits tilted on top. A sky-blue satchel hangs on the left hip on a strap across the front, stuffed with envelopes. Sturdy little boots with lime laces, short kraft arms, and a letter held out in the right hand.
- **Voice:** cheerful, quick, on schedule. Talks about the weather and the routes in the same breath ("light drizzle on Maker's Lane, so I took the long way"). Gives times like "ten past nine". Warm and chatty, never nosy.
- **Loves:** stamps, a dry morning, knowing which door is which, a letter with a hand-drawn heart on it. **Can't stand:** wet cardboard, a missing house number, a letter with no name on it.
- **Quirks:** every few seconds of idling Parcel checks the satchel: an envelope pops up, Parcel reads the address, nods, and tucks it back. Carries a different letter every time. On the mail round 8:00 to 12:00.
- **With the two of you:** hands you a note from the other one before saying hello, and always waits to see you read it. Calls the pair of you "the two-door house".
- **Relationships:** Fold, their flatmate, paints the signs; Parcel reads every one out loud on the round. Bunbun saves them a warm roll at the Snack Shack. Tock counts the letters with them, and they never agree on the total.

## Fold · Sign painter
- **Look:** an origami crane come to life, built from flat paper facets with crisp creases. Wings are lime washi paper with wave arcs and dots, each in three panels; the outer panel hinges on a crease. Two thin folded legs with a bent knee and flat triangle feet, a pointed paper tail, and a tiny paintbrush tucked under one wing.

- **Loves:** a clean crease, the corner of a fresh sheet, painting the curl of a sign letter, giving away tiny paper gifts. **Can't stand:** rain, damp, being unfolded by accident, a dog-eared edge.
- **Quirks:** unfolds one wing, then refolds it neatly with a small crinkle and a nod. Now and then does a slow paper-flap stretch. Leaves small paper gifts (a boat, a frog, a heart) in people's pockets and pretends it was the wind.
- **Dream:** to paint one sign so well that nobody notices it is a sign, only the street looking happier.
- **With the two of you:** makes {n} and both of you a paper gift each visit and says the fold was "nothing". Asks which corner of the room you like best.
- **Relationships:**
  - **Parcel:** flatmate. Fold labels everything in the flat with lovely lettering; Parcel stamps over it.
  - **Stitch:** swaps scraps. Fold gives paper, Stitch gives thread, nobody keeps score.
  - **Bolt:** hires Fold for every shop sign and then asks for it "a bit bigger".

## Posy · Gardener
