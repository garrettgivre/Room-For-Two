# Shop exteriors: concept art prompts

Every shop has two images per floor:
- `<shop>-floorN.jpg`: the interior as it looks in the game.
- `<shop>-floorN-plan.jpg`: a top-down floor plan.

Give ChatGPT both images for a shop, plus the shared style block and that shop's block below.

How to read the plan images:
- **Entrance:** the front door is on the left wall. It's the door with the little "Town" sign.
- **Staff rooms:** the darker, greyed rooms are staff-only. They sit at the back of the building.
- **Upper floors:** a second floor is reached by the stairs (or the lift in the arcade).
- **Shape:** the exterior should follow the plan's footprint (the L-shapes, bays and steps), because it has to wrap around the real rooms.

## Shared style (paste first)

> Concept art for a building exterior in a cozy virtual-pet game called Room for Two. Style: glossy jelly-candy toy look, like soft vinyl or gummy candy, thick deep-purple outlines (#3B1273), a white sticker-style edge around the silhouette, tiny sugar speckles, soft rounded shapes and no sharp corners. Palette: pink #FF3D9A, lime #A6E22E, cyan #3EC8F2, sunshine yellow #FFD21F, orange #FF8A1C, grape #9B4DF2. A 3/4 isometric view of the whole building standing on a small square of grass, like a diorama piece, on a plain white background. The building's footprint and room layout must match the attached floor plan: same shape, same number of floors, entrance on the side shown. Whimsical and fun, a little bit Klasky Csupo, cozy not creepy. No text except the shop name on its sign. No four-point stars or sparkles (five-point stars are fine).

## Snack Shack
- **Owner:** Bunbun, a cream bunny baker in a chef's hat with floppy ears.
- **Rooms:**
  - an L-shaped bakery shop floor with a café corner (bistro tables);
  - a staff kitchen with ovens;
  - a staff pantry at the back.
- **Floors:** one.

> The Snack Shack, Bunbun the bunny baker's bakery and café. Make the building itself look delicious: a frosted layer-cake or cupcake silhouette, a striped candy awning over the front, a chimney shaped like a piping bag or cupcake puffing sweet steam, windows like cookie cutouts. Pink-and-white checker trim. The café corner could spill out with a tiny patio. The staff kitchen at the back looks more practical (vents, a back door).
