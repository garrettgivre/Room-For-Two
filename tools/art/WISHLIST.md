# Art wishlist

Images the app would like, with ChatGPT prompts. Generate each one on its own, as a 1024×1024 PNG with a transparent
background. Claude trims them, cuts out any leftover background, and converts them to WebP in `assets/icons/`.
The town map art comes later, once the layout is settled.

**House style (add this to every prompt):**
> Glossy jelly-candy sticker icon, chunky rounded shapes, thick deep-purple outline (#3B1273), a white sticker border
> around the whole thing, soft glossy highlights and tiny speckles, bright candy palette (pink #FF3D9A, lime #A6E22E,
> cyan #3EC8F2, sun yellow #FFD21F, orange #FF8A1C, grape #9B4DF2). Centered, front view, transparent background,
> no text, no letters, no drop shadow on the background. Never a four-point sparkle; five-point stars are fine.
